## Simplified workflow
When required frames are approved, Frames shows a direct Create motion CTA. It switches to Motion & sound inside the same shot, opens the creation controls, and chooses an appropriate workflow when there is no existing user-authored motion package:
- 1 frame → I2V
- 2 frames → MiniMax H3 FLF when available
- 3+ frames → MiniMax H3 multi-frame when available
